Mini Biography: Imran Khan

Imran Khan is a former cricketer, politician and philanthropist from Pakistan.

Early Life

He was born on 5 October,1952 in Lahore,Pakistan.

He studied at Aitchison college and later at Oxford University.

Important Achievements

He led Pakistan to win the 1992 Cricket World Cup.

He founded the Shakut Khanum Memorial Cancer Hospital.

His leadership is a great examplefor young people.

Correction Corner

Imran Khan was only a cricketer. He was also a a politician and philanthropist.

Interesting Examples

He encouraged students to become the 1st version of themselves.
